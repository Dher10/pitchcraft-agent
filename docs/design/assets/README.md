# Logos

The PitchCraft mark is a home plate holding the nine cells of the strike zone, with one cell lit: the spot where the pitch was called. Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-13 (direction A).

- `pitchcraft-mark-on-light.svg`: plate in `board` (#0E3B2C), cells in `on-board` (#F4F7F2), lit cell in `signal` (#FFC93C). For `ground` and `surface`.
- `pitchcraft-mark-on-board.svg`: plate in `signal`, cells in `board`, lit cell in `stitch` (#D7382C). For the header and any `board` fill.
- `pitchcraft-favicon.svg`: the on-board mark on a `board` tile with a 7px corner. Replaces `app/favicon.ico`.
- `pitchcraft-alt-b-tiles.svg`: direction B (the initials as two scoreboard tiles). Kept as the alternate; not used anywhere.

The wordmark is live text, not a file: PITCHCRAFT in Big Shoulders Display 800, capitals, letter-spacing 0.02em, 10px to the right of the mark; the mark is 1.15 times the cap height. Clear space on every side: one cell of the zone. Minimum mark size 16px. The lit cell is always the low outside corner. Do not recolor the cells, rotate the plate, or set the mark on a team color.
