### QA-02 — a dated run can publish future information

**Evidence / reproduction:** run `run_live_pipeline('2026-07-15')` with the schedule, `STATCAST` and `FUTURE_STATS` from `tests/test_as_of_end_to_end.py`; stub season dates and route schedule/stats responses through its `FakeResponse`. The real fetchers and real snapshot builder ran:

**Smallest suggested fix:** resolve a safe cutoff from the effective game date at the pipeline boundary, or reject dated runs without one; test both historical and daily callers. Resolve the documented D23 caching concern explicitly without allowing future data.
