PitchCraft v2.1.0: a new look and the pages that let you browse the 2026 postseason, one game at a time. No predictions, no betting.

## What changed since v2.0-a
- A new visual identity (design system v2): the scoreboard look, Big Shoulders Display, one header and footer on every page, light theme.
- `/` in the off season is the season in review: the World Series line score, the top three notable games and how PitchCraft works. In season, the daily view is unchanged.
- `/game/<gamePk>`: one page per game after it was played, with the final score, why it is notable, each starter's final line from the box score, and the report as it was published (or written after the game), labelled by its origin.
- `/archive`: filter by team, pick a day or a range on a calendar, and filter by reason. It is rendered on the server, so it works without JavaScript.
- `/notable`: the 30 notable games of the 2026 postseason, with the reasons as filters and a plain list of how games are chosen.
- The archive behind these pages: final results, one file per game, notable duels and the final starter line, built from public MLB data under a fixed request budget (40 games).
- Security headers and a nonce-based Content Security Policy.
- The LLM writer stays off in production; reports are rules-based. The evaluation behind that choice is in `docs/results-planB.md`.

## QA
`docs/qa/` in this repository holds every gate report, including the failed ones and how each finding was fixed. For this release: the archive data audit (`docs/qa/R4-data-audit-2026-11-14.md`), the release 3 gate (`docs/qa/QA-release-3-2026-11-07.md`), a clean-context review of every commit, and a browser check of nine page states at 375, 430 and 1280 px.

## Not in this release
- The after-the-game layer (pitches, Game Score, verdicts, answers), team and pitcher pages, Today in season and the Spanish version.
